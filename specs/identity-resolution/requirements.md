  publisher descriptions, review records and source hashes; no direct native-ID
  promotion or production changes.

  accessible source evidence. Distinguish public manual source reading from a
  licensed bulk statement collector. No authentication/restriction bypass or full

  pending candidate, not a guessed or silently inserted catalog entry.
