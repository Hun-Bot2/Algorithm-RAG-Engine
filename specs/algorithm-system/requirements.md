# Full-catalog learning research requirements

Current user instruction supersedes the approval pauses of earlier statement
research. Execute the four checkpoints sequentially with independent review; do
not fabricate missing evidence, bypass access restrictions, deploy, change
production behavior or promote a model. Preserve all frozen baselines and labels.

- CP1 SHALL reconcile unique platform identities, Premium exclusion evidence,
  native difficulties, statement rights/coverage and duplicates; normalize original
  tags with hierarchy, confidence, ambiguous/unmapped states and source retention.
- CP2 SHALL use documented public Codeforces metadata and community AtCoder
  difficulty data without credentials. Negative/raw estimates remain preserved.
  Analyze distributions and empirical quantiles with ties and uncertainty; native
  categorical ranks do not establish numerical cross-platform equality. Fit a
  monotonic/ordinal model only with sufficient independent difficulty judgments
  and frozen calibration/selection/test membership, never on final evaluation pairs.
- Eligible publisher-licensed statements MAY be imported under the actual data
  license and retained third-party conditions. Distinguish code licenses from
  dataset licenses and publisher-copy provenance from original archives. No direct
  restricted platform scraping or automatic release of quarantined LeetCode text.
- Collect only statement/identity/public-sample metadata, not unrelated submission
  code archives, hidden/generated tests, editorial solutions or model training data.
  Native joins require authoritative IDs/URLs; names alone produce review candidates.
- CP3 SHALL compare identical eligible query/candidate sets for word/character
  TF-IDF, MiniLM title, title+statement prefix, full-statement chunking, raw tags,
  hierarchical taxonomy and hybrid methods, with explicit language/tag/source
  coverage. A 256-token prefix MUST NOT be called full-statement coverage.
- Retrieval SHALL be exact blockwise NumPy with stable ties and Top10/20/50 saved
  privately. Report P@K/nDCG, judged coverage, topic/difficulty diagnostics, hard
  negatives, hubness, diversity and query-bootstrap intervals. Unjudged is not zero.
- Preserve the 3,647 metadata-pooled pair records as AI-reviewed/abstained evidence,
  not human gold or newly statement-grounded labels. New methods SHALL contribute
  a stratified human-review queue without fabricated reviews or outcomes.
- CP4 compression SHALL follow the 384D full-statement baseline, comparing original,
  PCA/SVD 256/128/64 and random projection when dimensional rank permits. Report
  quality, neighbors, latency, memory/disk, fit population and leakage limits.
- An adaptive architecture SHALL distinguish equivalent practice, progressive
  challenge, prerequisite repair and spaced review using explicit learner evidence,
  native/provisional difficulty, mastery, prerequisites and exploration. No RL.
