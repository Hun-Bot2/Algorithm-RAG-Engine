How to read this. Positions and scores come from a MiniLM embedding of each problem's title only. They show how alike two titles read — not that two problems share an algorithm, invariant or difficulty. Platform tags and the AI-reviewed taxonomy are shown as separate evidence and were never used to place points or rank neighbors. AtCoder publishes no tags. 2D maps distort distances; see the fidelity numbers.

Limits
  • Title similarity is a weak proxy: generic or narrative titles match by wording, not technique.
  • Non-Latin titles (mostly Japanese AtCoder) are embedded by an English-only model: marked degraded/unreliable.
  • Neighbors are exact in the 384-d space; map distances are not. Compare the three projections before reading structure into any one.
  • Judged pairs cover only a small, selected set of pairs; absence of a judgment is unknown, not negative.
